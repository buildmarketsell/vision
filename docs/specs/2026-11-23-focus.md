# Focus, meeting heads-up and rollover

Agreed with Dan on 2026-11-23 (grill session, after looking at Black Hole, getblackhole.app, MIT). Words as in `GLOSSARY.md`.

## Problem Statement

VISION knows Dan's to-dos and calendar, but only while its window is in front. When he works in another app, nothing tells him how long he has been on a to-do, that a call starts in five minutes, or where the call link is. A planned to-do whose time ran out just sits flagged "at risk" until he notices.

## Solution

- **Focus**: start a countdown (15 / 25 / 50 min or any length) or a stopwatch on a to-do, or on nothing. It shows in the Mac menu bar and in an island under the notch, both visible from any app. When a countdown ends, a notification with a sound and a check-in: Done, +5 min, or Not yet (how much more it needs, which goes to auto-schedule).
- **Heads-up**: five minutes before a meeting, a notification, and the menu bar and island count down to it, with Join when the meeting has a Meet, Zoom or Teams link.
- **Rollover**: a planned to-do whose planned time has passed without being ticked off gets another block at the next free moment, as often as needed.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Focus clock** (pure, shared): the focus state and every change to it (start, pause, resume, add time, stop, the countdown ending, check-in answers) as functions of state and time. Elapsed time is computed from timestamps, never counted by ticks, so a busy or sleeping app never drifts.
- **Glance** (pure, shared): one function turns the focus state, the next meeting and the time into what the menu bar and the island show (label, title, progress, buttons). Menu bar and island draw from it, so they never disagree. Picks the meeting over a focus during a heads-up.
- **Meetings** (pure, shared): which events are meetings, which one is in heads-up now (from five minutes before its start until ten minutes after, or its end if sooner), and its join link. The join link comes from Google's Meet link or conference data, else a Meet, Zoom or Teams address in the location or description; only `https` links to those hosts are ever opened.
- **Focus feature, main side**: owns the focus state (saved in `userData/focus.json` through the document store, with finished focuses as a log for stats), ticks once a second only while something shows, sends notifications, pauses on sleep, draws the menu bar item, and moves the island window. It reaches to-dos through the shared to-do list and calendar events and plan blocks through a small calendar bridge in `src/main`, never by importing the Calendar feature.
- **Island window**: a frameless, see-through window that sits on top of everything, on all Spaces and over full-screen apps, never takes focus, and lets clicks through except over the island itself. On a Mac with a notch it wraps the notch (time on the right, a progress ring on the left); otherwise it sits at the top center.
- **Not yet**: sets the to-do's plan so exactly the asked minutes are still to plan from now on (time already planned and passed, and the part of a block under way that has passed, are kept). A to-do without a plan gets the To-do page's default plan.
- **Rollover**: in plan progress, a planned open to-do whose past blocks cover its whole plan and that has no block under way gets another piece of its smallest piece (30 minutes when it is never split). The "time used" risk goes away.
- **IPC**: `focus:state`, `focus:start`, `focus:act`, `focus:changed`, `focus:join`, `focus:setIsland`. Every argument is checked in main.

## Testing Decisions

- Tests check behaviour through each module's interface, with fixed times, never the insides.
- Unit (vitest): focus clock (start, pause, resume, add, stop, countdown ends exactly once, sleep pause, check-in answers), glance (what shows when, meeting wins), meetings (which events count, heads-up window, join links and refused links), plan progress (rollover and Not yet).
- End to end (Playwright, headless): start a focus from a to-do, see the to-do move to In progress and the island window show the time; finish a countdown, answer Done and see the to-do ticked; heads-up for a fake meeting with a join link. Prior art: `todo-calendar.spec.ts`, `autoSchedule.spec.ts`, the fake Google.
- The menu bar item is checked through the glance and its menu model; the real tray isn't created in headless runs.

## Out of Scope

- Weekly stats page (next round, from the saved focus log).
- Counting focus time against a to-do's planned time.
- Spotify, a daily notepad, the AI tunnel from Black Hole.
- Moving to-dos without a plan.

## Further Notes

- Mac notifications with buttons need a signed app; VISION is signed ad-hoc, so the buttons live in the island and the menu bar and a notification click brings them up.
